## Known evasion log

- Brands absent from the curated dataset are not detected reliably.
- Whole-script homographs without mixed scripts may evade the current rule.
- Keyword-only phishing without a brand signal can evade static brand rules.
- A legitimate compromised site can look benign in static analysis.

## Scope and limitations

Current sample size: 5 benign and 40 adversarial. The plan requires at least 40 of each and dated verification of benign entries; this sample does not meet that acceptance threshold.
Coverage and confidence are from the three local checks in this offline harness, not from a full API report. Redirect, password, DNS, RDAP, external reputation, Redis, Docker, and live PostgreSQL behaviors are not measured here. Suspicious cases and any corpus false positives require human disposition before the plan's false-positive criteria can pass.
